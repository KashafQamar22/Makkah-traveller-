Important Information Before Your Visit

Access to some sacred sites may be restricted during Hajj or at other times. Museum opening hours, entry requirements and transport access can change, so confirm details before travelling. Jabal Al-Noor and Jabal Thawr involve strenuous uphill climbs to reach their caves; visitors should consider their fitness and safety. A vehicle can take you to the destination area but may not be able to reach the cave itself.
